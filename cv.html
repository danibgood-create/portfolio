01 ABOUT ME

Summary

Although I studied graphic design, I have always focused my career on the web. Over almost 20 years of professional experience, I have worked in different environments (in-house, agencies and consulting firms) and experienced first-hand how the industry has evolved.

I spent most of my career as a UX Designer at Flat 101, one of the leading companies in Spain, where my versatility and experience led me to take on management responsibilities.

I currently work as a Senior Digital Product Designer at Accenture, where I also take on some Team Lead responsibilities.

Highlights

  • Selected for “Future Leaders 2023”, the talent program for managers of Flat 101, led by Alquimia and held at Indra’s facilities.
  • Promoted to Design Manager in 2023, leading teams of 6 to 12 people, getting improvements in technical efficiency, operations, and team engagement.
  • Member of Flat 101 Design Department Management Committee since 2022, contributing to the strategic planning and key decision-making for the area.
  • Design lead on major projects for clients such as MAPFRE, L’Oréal, ALSA, and IESE Publishing.
  • Rethinking of the heuristic analysis process, optimizing workflow and increasing the identification of actionable insights.
  • Development of a system for flow organization and page structure in Figma, that significantly reduced the onboarding time for stakeholders.
  • Creation of a writing guide for “ami”, MAPFRE’s virtual assistant, where define its voice, tone, formats, and editorial rules.
  • UX/UI design and functional documentation of a conversational interface for GSK, as part of a POC on agent orchestration to optimise supply chain processes.
  • Design and evolution of GSK’s internal tool for viewing KPI-based reports across different business units.
  • Development of the Design System for the new Junta de Extremadura website, defining foundations, variables, components and documentation, with the support of Claude connected to Figma.
